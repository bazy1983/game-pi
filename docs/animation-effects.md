# Animation effects

Ambient effects for ✨ Lights, which play on the strip whenever nobody is
playing. Every effect takes its colors from the chosen palette (Rainbow,
Fire, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Party, Ice, or your own gradient), and has a
speed and brightness setting. LED 0 is the bottom of the strip.

## On the unit now

| Effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| Flow | The palette scrolls up the strip, twice along its length. |
| Aurora | Slow overlapping waves of light and color, like northern lights. |
| Fire | Flames rise from the bottom of the strip, cool colors low and hot tips higher. |
| Twinkle | LEDs light up here and there in palette colors and fade away. |
| Flash sparkle *(new)* | The palette glows softly over the strip while white sparks flash on it. |
| Meteor | Meteors fall from the top, leaving sparkling trails. |
| Scanner *(new)* | A glowing eye sweeps up and down with a fading tail (K.I.T.T.), drifting through the palette. |
| Fireworks | Rockets rise and burst into sparks that spread, fall and shimmer. |
| Ripples | Drops land at random spots and send rings out both ways. |
| Bouncing balls | Four balls drop and bounce on the bottom of the strip. |
| Chase | Bands of light march up the strip, each a palette color. |
| Running lights *(new)* | Smooth sine waves of light run up the strip over the palette. |
| Color wipe *(new)* | The next palette color fills the strip from the bottom, the one after it from the top, and so on. |
| Breathe | The whole strip slowly brightens and dims, drifting through the palette. |
| Still | The palette spread over the strip, not moving. With one color, a solid color. |
| Sound level | A bar rises with the sound from the microphone; a dot marks the recent peak. |

Games also have their own animations (confetti, fireworks, the red fill when
you lose), which aren't in this list.

### Good next picks

- **Dual Scan:** two Scanner eyes crossing; cheap to add on top of Scanner.
- **Fire Flicker:** the whole strip flickering like a candle or torch, a
  calmer alternative to Fire.
- **Multi Dynamic / Single Dynamic:** random palette colors per LED,
  changing all at once or one at a time.
- **Holiday presets:** Christmas, Halloween and Circus as ready-made saved
  gradients, so they're one tap away instead of hand-built.
